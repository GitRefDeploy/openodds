OpenOdds logo concepts

Four marks. Each is shown at 16, 24, 32, 64 and 128px, then in the sidebar brand block at its real 216px width and 32px tile. Judge them at 16px first — that is where a mark usually falls apart.

1 · Odds line in a tile — recommended

“The line that goes up”

Filled accent tile with a rising line and its end point. Reads at 16px, and says the one thing the app is for.

2 · Two rings — the OO monogram

The current badge, drawn properly: two overlapping rings, the second lighter, suggesting two sides of a market. Least change, most recognisable as the app that already exists.

3 · Ascending bars — profit as a shape

Three round-capped bars stepping up. The most literal reading of a profit and loss app, and the most conventional — it could belong to any tracker.

4 · Bet slip — the thing you are replacing

A ticket with a notched edge and a printed line. The most specific to betting, and the weakest at 16px, where the notches close up.

How to choose

Say the number and I will wire it in everywhere: the sidebar, the window and installer icons (src-tauri/icons/), and the browser favicon. The SVG source lands in src/assets/, and re-rendering the icons is one command, so changing your mind later costs a minute rather than a rework. Concept 1 is the one I would pick: it survives 16px, it says profit rather than betting paraphernalia, and it does not lean on the accent colour for its shape, so it still reads in a light theme.
